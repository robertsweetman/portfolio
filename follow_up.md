# Follow-up Evidence Review

This review identifies gaps in the source material used for the portfolio pages. It does not add evidence or claim that work was completed when the source only describes a proposal. Sections noted below need further source evidence before a confident B-level assessment.

## Project 1 - Cybersecurity & Software Development

- **K12**: Evidence is limited to the tender data flow and database state. Further evidence of data analysis or management outcomes may be needed for a confident B.
- **S9**: The source primarily recommends security and resilience changes. Evidence that those changes were implemented and tested is not present; this may not demonstrate B-level application.
- **B4 and B7**: The template labels both with the criterion "Explains how teams work effectively ... using up to date awareness of trends and innovations". The existing evidence was selected for continuous professional development (B4) and awareness of trends (B7), so it does not directly address how teams work effectively. Further source evidence on teamwork is needed for B.
- **Images**: No source image directly illustrates K12 or B4. B7's trend evidence is text and quotations; no related source image is available.

## Project 2 - Driving Business Value with Data Engineering

- **S10**: The source includes a proof-of-concept design, code examples, logs and test evidence. Additional evidence of the deployed product and measured end-to-end outcomes would make implementation and debugging stronger for B.
- **K14**: Business benefits and rollout measures are projected; realized business outcomes are not documented in the source.
- **Images**: The source provides diagrams, dashboard screenshots and test evidence for the relevant sections. The data-product ERD is a Mermaid diagram rather than a separate image.

## Project 3 - Advancing your Data Strategy and Governance

- **K9**: The change-management material describes Kotter's steps and stakeholders, but evidence of applying the steps or resulting change is limited.
- **K10**: The risk assessment and mitigation are concise; evidence of monitoring or outcomes is not present.
- **K20**: The inclusion and sustainability benefits are proposed rather than measured. This section may not demonstrate B-level impact without additional source evidence, and the template's Distinction criterion (evaluating the impact of the organisation's sustainable practices) is not met.
- **Images**: No suitable source image is available for K9, K10 or K20; those source sections are text and tables.

## Project 4 - Integrating Machine Learning and AI to Drive Business Value

- **K19, S15 and B2**: The source discusses ethical and regulatory considerations, but evidence of formal approval or implementation of the cited standards is limited. These sections may not demonstrate B-level application.
- **K24**: Requirements and design considerations are present, but implementation evidence across non-functional and security requirements is limited.
- **S4**: The source documents development and debugging approaches, but further code-level evidence of testing and debugging outcomes may be needed for B.
- **K19 and S15 (Distinction)**: The template asks for justification of the applied standards; the source states considerations rather than justifying the choices.
- **Images**: No source image directly supports K19, K24, S15 or B2. Model-analysis images (data preparation, decision tree, linear regression, tokenizer comparison, final model, enhanced-content comparison) support S4.

## Project 5 - Managing Software Transformation Projects

- **S23**: The business case, business analysis methodology and justification statement cite professional and academic sources, but evidence that this research led to improvements in the organisation is limited.
- **B7**: The project charter, risk management plan and communication plan apply organisational models (MoSCoW, BIA/CBA, RACI, CATWOE, ELM). Evidence of their effect on team working and outcomes is limited, so this may not reach B.
- **Images**: The source Markdown has no embedded project-evidence images. `sources/image.png` in the source repository is a word-count/layout guide, not evidence for a learning outcome, so it has not been used as one.

## Project 6 - Cloud Computing and Scalable Architectures

- **K28**: Tools and teamwork practices are described; further evidence of their effectiveness in team outcomes could strengthen a B-level assessment.
- **Images**: No source image directly illustrates K28. The resource map supports K16 and K24; the application screenshots and May 2026 cost chart support S20.
